# DostAI - Pakistan's WhatsApp AI Assistant

🇵🇰 Pakistan's first proactive AI assistant with full audio support for WhatsApp.

- 🎙️ Voice messages in Urdu/English
- 💳 JazzCash & Easypaisa payments
- 📧 Email & Calendar management
- 🔔 Proactive reminders
- 🚗 Careem booking
- 🍕 Foodpanda orders
